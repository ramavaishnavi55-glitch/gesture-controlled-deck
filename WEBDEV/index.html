02 / How It Works

Under the Hood

🤖

AI Model

MediaPipe Hand Landmarker detects 21 key points (landmarks) on your hand in every video frame.

📐

Gesture Math

We compare the X/Y positions of fingertip landmarks to their base joints to decide if a finger is up or down.

⏱️

Cooldown Timer

A 1.5-second lock prevents the same gesture from firing multiple times on a single movement.

🪞

Mirror View

The webcam feed is CSS-flipped so it feels like a mirror — left on screen = left in real life.
